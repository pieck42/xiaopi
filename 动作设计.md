# 小皮动作设计 · 02

使用内置 imagegen 生成两套透明动作图，保留原有像素风与角色配色。每种角色有五种状态，每种状态两帧，共 20 帧。生成的原始动作图完整保存在 assets 中，应用在运行时读取角色帧；元数据同时供桌面版与网页使用。

| 状态 | 设计 | 循环节奏 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 待机 | 安静坐好，睁眼与闭眼变化 | 睁眼 2.7 秒，闭眼 0.16 秒 |
| 倾听 | 把爪子举到耳旁，歪头靠近 | 每帧 0.7 秒 |
| 思考 | 托腮、抬眼，改变视线与头部角度 | 每帧 1 秒 |
| 回答 | 张嘴与小圆嘴型交替，伸爪示意 | 两帧分别 0.23 秒、0.19 秒 |
| 开心 | 眯眼笑、挥爪庆祝，小龙展开翅膀 | 每帧 0.3 秒，主角另有轻跳 |

这是两帧循环的像素动作；还没有连续多帧过渡、真实语音嘴型同步或语音识别。两帧之间存在少量绘制差异，当前通过统一比例和地面基线减少跳动。

素材：`assets/dragon-actions-v2.png`、`assets/fox-actions-v2.png`。运行时通过 `assets/animations.json` 的帧范围和透明连通区域种子分离角色，避免相邻耳朵或尾巴混入画面。原始单姿势素材保留。

## 小龙生成提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art animation SPRITE SHEET on genuine transparent background. Reference image is the character design reference, preserve this cute mint baby dragon's identity, horns, wings, palette, cream belly and proportions. Make EXACTLY TEN full-body sprites, in an exact evenly spaced grid of FIVE columns and TWO rows. Entire canvas aspect ratio 5:2, each cell square and same size. No labels, no text, no panels, no borders, no shadows, no scenery. No extra sprites. Every sprite centered in its own cell with generous 10% padding, feet baseline at 88% of its cell. Consistent scale and position across all ten sprites, all facing three-quarter right. Hard-edged pixel art as an enlarged 64x64 game sprite, flat limited palette, dark pixel outlines. COLUMNS LEFT TO RIGHT are five DISTINCT emotional/action states; TWO ROWS are the two animation frames of each column. Column 1 IDLE: row1 relaxed sitting mouth closed eyes open wings folded; row2 same exact sitting pose but eyes closed blinking. Column 2 LISTENING: row1 leaning forward head cocked, one hand cupped beside ear and eyes wide attentive mouth closed; row2 slightly further head tilt with hand still cupped by ear. Column 3 THINKING: row1 seated, one hand on chin, narrowed contemplative eyes looking upward, mouth small; row2 hand still on chin, eyes looking other upward direction and head slightly tilted. Column 4 SPEAKING: row1 standing, mouth visibly wide open talking, one hand extended outward explaining; row2 same standing position mouth small rounded and explaining hand bent slightly inward. Column 5 HAPPY: row1 exuberant closed crescent eyes, broad smile, both hands raised, wings spread; row2 same delighted eyes and smile, hands and wings flapping downward slightly. Each column must be obviously different from others even when seen at small size. NOT ten duplicates of the original open-mouth pose. Transparent alpha background, aligned sprite sheet ready for runtime grid slicing. All body parts fully contained in their cell.
